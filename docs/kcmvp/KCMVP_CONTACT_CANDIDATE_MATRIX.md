## Selection criteria

The project owner must record the decision against at least:

- whether informal scope consultation is offered before a test application;
- supported secure communication method and material-handling instructions;
- software-module and target-operating-environment experience;
- entropy/DRBG and module-integrity consultation coverage;
- expected first-contact information and prohibited attachments;
- scheduling, commercial, legal, and confidentiality prerequisites;
- whether the institution expects use of the online management/pre-validation service.

No candidate is selected by this document. Contact requires a named owner decision after the independent human rehearsal records are complete.
